# WUP 2025 outcome-lineage audit

## Finding

The prior WUP forecast pipeline treated every F21 city population year through 2025 as an `estimate`. That matches the WUP publication-level convention, which describes 1950–2025 as estimates, but it is too coarse for empirical forecast evaluation.

The WUP 2025 methodological report distinguishes the underlying DEGURBA population lineage:

- 1950–1975: historical backcasting;
- 1975–2020: reference estimates derived from GHS-WUP-POP / GHS-POP and remote-sensing inputs, with national-total reconciliation; and
- 2020–2100: projections generated by the CRISP model. The underlying GHS-WUP-POP product is described as observationally consistent through 2020 and projected thereafter.

Therefore the 2025 city endpoint used by a forecast origin of 2020 is a model-generated CRISP projection even though WUP's public table labels the broader 1950–2025 period as estimates.

## Consequence for existing results

The existing 2020-origin WUP comparison (`2020 -> 2025`) is not an observed/reference-estimate forecast test. Its previously reported persistence reversal, influence diagnostics, balanced-cohort checks, bootstrap inference, and locked-origin comparison can still describe how the WUP/CRISP projection machinery behaves, but they cannot be used as evidence that actual city growth between 2020 and 2025 mean-reverted in the same way.

This is a more parsimonious threat than the existing country/city influence checks because those checks condition on the same projected endpoint. They test sample influence, not endpoint lineage.

## Remediation

`classify_wup_city_population_lineage` now preserves the publisher label in `publisher_observation_type` while setting the forecast-evaluation `observation_type` according to empirical lineage. The default `build_forecast_intervals` gate therefore excludes 2025 as an outcome and the observed WUP scoring path ends at the 2015 origin with a 2020 endpoint.

The prior 2020 diagnostics are retained only through an explicit opt-in to projection outcomes and are labelled:

- `outcome_empirical_lineage = crisp_projection`;
- `headline_eligible = false`; and
- `interpretation = 2020-to-2025 WUP projection sensitivity; not an observed-outcome forecast test`.

The dynamic hierarchy runner likewise excludes the 2020-to-2025 interval from its empirical common sample.

## What this does not establish

The 1975–2020 reference estimates are not direct city census counts. They are spatially modelled population estimates built from census/administrative population inputs, GHS-POP disaggregation, remote sensing and national-total reconciliation. Accordingly, removing the 2025 CRISP projection fixes the clearest endpoint-lineage error but does not prove that earlier WUP city growth is measurement-error-free.

A stronger next test is country-level stratification by the recency and granularity of the population inputs underlying GHS-POP. The relevant question is whether persistence and mean reversion differ between countries whose 2020 reference estimate is closely anchored to recent census/administrative inputs and countries whose 2020 grid is more heavily extrapolated or modelled.

## F01/F21 national comparator audit

The national Cities-category baseline does not subtract F01 from F21. It takes F01 national Cities-category growth and, for the leave-city-out variant, subtracts the focal F21 city population from both F01 national endpoints before computing growth. The code correctly keeps both national endpoints at or before the forecast origin and records the inclusive and leave-city-out variants separately.

However, the subtraction relies on an explicit assumption that the focal F21 city's population is a component of the corresponding F01 national Cities-category total under the same revision. The code already records `national_focal_component_membership_assumed = true`. That assumption is plausible because both products use the same DEGURBA revision, but it is not independently demonstrated by a city-to-national-component reconciliation table. The leave-city-out national comparator should therefore remain a robustness baseline, not a literal accounting identity, until component membership is validated.

## Status

This audit invalidates the prior treatment of the 2020-origin WUP result as an observed-outcome headline. Existing registered result files containing the 2020 origin should be treated as superseded for headline interpretation until the WUP baseline and hierarchy workflows are rerun with the corrected lineage classification and their manifests are regenerated.
